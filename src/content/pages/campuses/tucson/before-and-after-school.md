AmeriSchools Academy is proud to offer before and after school care service to our families provided by Capstone Quest Academy. Capstone Quest Academy Kids Club welcomes AmeriSchools students, Kindergarten through 8th Level, and integrates the curriculum into games, physical movement and centers to keep your children learning and having fun!

**BEFORE SCHOOL PROGRAMS**

Monday – Friday: 6:45 – 8:00 AM

**AFTER SCHOOL PROGRAMS**

Monday – Friday: End of School – 6:00 PM
