AmeriSchools is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information gathered through our website.

What Information We Collect

We may collect basic information such as your name, phone number, email address, or other details when you:

-   Fill out a contact or inquiry form
-   Sign up to receive newsletters or updates
-   Interact with our website through cookies and analytics tools

We do **not** collect financial information or Social Security numbers through this website.

#### How We Use Your Information

We may use collected information to:

-   Respond to your inquiries or requests
-   Send updates about school events, enrollment, or announcements
-   Improve our website and services through usage data and analytics

#### Use of Cookies & Tracking

We use cookies to enhance user experience and understand how visitors interact with our website. This may include:

-   Google Analytics
-   Meta Pixel (Facebook/Instagram Ads)
-   Other tools for website performance tracking and marketing

You can control cookie preferences through your browser settings. Disabling cookies may impact some website functionality.

#### Google & Meta Advertising

We may use Google Ads and Meta (Facebook/Instagram) advertising features, such as:

-   Remarketing
-   Conversion tracking
-   Interest-based advertising

These platforms may use cookies or similar technologies to serve ads based on past visits to our website. You can opt out of Google’s use of cookies via Google Ads Settings and out of Meta’s ad targeting via your Facebook Ad Preferences.

#### Third-Party Sharing

We do **not** sell or trade your personal information.

#### FERPA Notice

AmeriSchools abides by the Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act (FERPA) regarding the protection and use of student education records. Any information regarding students is handled in accordance with FERPA regulations and is not collected through this public website.
