Our Early Bird Club before school program is available Monday through Friday from 6:30 AM-7:45 AM. After School programs are provided through the Phoenix Afterschool Centers (PAC). Registration for PAC is available at the following site:

[www.phoenix.gov/parks/pac](http://www.phoenix.gov/parks/pac)

PAC is available every day school is in session, including 1/2 days. Registration is available online or in person at one of the Phoenix community centers.

**BEFORE SCHOOL:** 6:30 AM – 7:45 AM

**AFTER SCHOOL PAC PROGRAM:** End of School – 6:00 PM
