As one of the nation’s first charter schools, founded in 1996, we are proud of our nearly 25-year history of making a difference in Arizona’s charter school system, which is the largest of any state and represents 25% of all charter schools in America.

Today, we are proud of our four campuses that serve Arizona learning communities. The Phoenix school caters to Kindergarten through 8th grade. The Tucson campus includes students from pre-kindergarten through 8th grade. We have two campuses in Yuma, Arizona. Yuma North campus is for pre-kindergarten through 5th grade students, and Yuma Middle is our dedicated middle school campus for grades 6th -8th grade.

At AmeriSchools we see our surrounding community as a laboratory where students get the chance to experience an education outside of our school’s walls. These explorations have included visits to the Grand Canyon, Pima Air and Space Museum, and Catalina Island Marine Institute.

### Amerischools Phoenix Campus K-8

At AmeriSchools Academy™ Phoenix, we know it takes a village to create an awesome learning journey. That’s why we’re all about teaming up with parents, teachers, and students to make school a win for everyone. Our K-8 program is built on innovative teaching methods and open communication, which is why so many Phoenix families choose us for their kids’ education.

Amerischools Phoenix campus offers, in addition to an excellent K-8 education:

-   FREE Breakfast & Lunch to all students
-   Explorations (Field Trips)
-   Clubs
-   Athletics
-   Before & Afterschool Care
