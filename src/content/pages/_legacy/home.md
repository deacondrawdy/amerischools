### THE AMERISCHOOLS DIFFERENCE

AmeriSchools is an unsurpassed tuition-free, exceptional learning community of professionals dedicated to providing a clear choice in education for children in grades K-8, with highly- experienced, qualified teachers and administrators as part of our program. AmeriSchools Academy™ strives to create and maintain a safe and inviting learning community to best support students and their families. Students at AmeriSchools are provided opportunities to enrich their schooling experience through a variety of clubs, committees, and athletic teams.

As one of the best charter schools in Arizona, our goal is to develop lifelong learners who are compassionate, culturally sensitive, effective, and responsible citizens. To join our amazing school community, enroll in one of our locations throughout Arizona offering elementary and middle school grade levels: Phoenix (K-8), Tucson (K-8), and Yuma (K-8).
