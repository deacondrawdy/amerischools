Work with Us!

AmeriSchools Academy™ is always seeking highly motivated candidates in teaching positions at any of our four campuses. Candidates must have a current and valid fingerprint clearance, Bachelors degree and passed the AEPA or NES. Candidates must possess a love of children, the ability to create engaging lessons and be motivated to meet each child’s academic needs. Our small school and class sizes allow for responsive teaching using best practices, embedded professional time and mentoring. Please click the link to apply for open positions in your area.
