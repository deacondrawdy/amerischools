### AmeriSchools Academy phoenix is a caring community with teachers and staff that facilitate opportunities to problem solve through enriched hands-on experiences.

The innovative teaching and open communication between our K-8 program staff, teachers, students and parents is the reason that AmeriSchools Phoenix is the Charter School Phoenix residents have chosen for their children. AmeriSchools in Phoenix has established the Kids at Hope philosophy with staff, students, and parents, which is, “All Children are Capable of Success, No exceptions!”

AmeriSchools Academy is Proud to Offer Full-Day Kindergarten! Kindergarten is an important milestone for students. At AmeriSchools Phoenix, strong academic programs build a foundation for lifelong learning. Parents of children who turn 5 years of age before September 1 may register their child at the school.

#### Phoenix

###### k-8

AmeriSchools Phoenix offers unique programs during the school day to enrich and supplement our academic curriculum. Such programs include Art, Music, Typing, Yoga, Systems Thinking and Problem Solving Learning Labs, Agricultural and Food Production through Aquaponics, and other Special Recreation hands-on activities.
