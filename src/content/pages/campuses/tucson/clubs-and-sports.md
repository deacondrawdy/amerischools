At AmeriSchools Academy – Country Club we believe after school clubs can have a positive impact on a student’s social and emotional development. We offer a wide variety of after school clubs to meet the passions and interests of all students.

This Semester our list of after school clubs include:

Coloring Club, Elementary Cross Country, Drama Club, Wii Gaming Club, Reader’s Theater, Game On!, Coding and Robotics, Chess Club, Drum for Peace, Yoga, Cooking Club, Young Entrepreneurs, Go Noodle Dance, Basketball Camp, Set Design, UNO Club, Girls Health and Fitness.

Staying active and participating in sports programs are important to any child’s development. Many important skills such as teamwork, full effort, and sportsmanship are developed through participation on our sports teams. At AmeriSchools Academy we offer various opportunities for students to maintain a healthy lifestyle through sports. Sports options for the 2019-2020 school year are as follows.

##### Middle School Students  
(6th – 8th grade)

-   Flag Football
-   Soccer
-   Volleyball

##### Elementary Students  
(1st – 5th Grade)

-   Cross Country
-   Flag Football
-   Basketball
-   Soccer
