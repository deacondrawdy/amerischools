Our students in grades 6-8 have the opportunity to join in our afterschool clubs, while our students in grades 4-6 have the opportunity to participate in after-school sports. Students must portray good citizenship and behavior to participate.

##### Clubs (6th-7th Grade)

-   Recreation Club
-   Cooking
-   Painting
-   Cheerleading
-   Computers
-   Gooey Science
-   Leadership
-   Crafts
-   Board Games
-   Chess

###### These club change and new ones may be available when signing up.

##### Sports (4th – 6th Grade)

-   **FALL** – Boys Flag Football, Girls Basketball
-   **WINTER** – Boys Basketball, Girls Softball
-   **SPRING** – Girls Volleyball
