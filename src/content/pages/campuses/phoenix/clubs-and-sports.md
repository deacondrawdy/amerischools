Our students in grades K-8 have the opportunity to participate in after-school sports. Students must maintain in good academic standing in order to participate.

##### Middle School Students  
(5th – 8th Grade)

-   **FALL** – Boys Flag Football, Girls Volleyball
-   **WINTER** – Boys Basketball and Girls Basketball
-   **SPRING** – Co-ed Soccer and Track (One-time Track Meet)

##### Elementary Students  
(1st – 4th Grade)

-   **WINTER AND SPRING** – Co-ed Soccer
-   **WINTER** – Cone Ball (Similar to Dodgeball)
