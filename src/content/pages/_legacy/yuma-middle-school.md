### Yuma Middle School is a unique and caring community with teachers and staff who facilitate opportunities to problem solve through hands-on experiences.

Finding Yuma schools that highlight each student’s unique gifts and talents can be difficult. AmeriSchools Academy is identified among the Yuma Charter Schools as a top-performing school amongst state, staff, and parents, and is rated among the top schools in Yuma County. Our school personalizes each child’s learning plan to give each child their own inspired learning experience. The list of core classes taught, as well as the additional lessons provided by amazing teachers, will give your child an education you can trust.

#### **1\. High School Preparation**

Strong academics and structured learning are intentionally designed to prepare students for a confident transition into high school. Students develop study skills, accountability, and leadership habits that support long-term success.

#### **2\. Safe & Structured Learning Environment**

A safe, respectful environment where clear expectations and positive behavior are consistently reinforced – creating a focused, distraction-free space where students can thrive.

#### **3\. Small School Community**

The low teacher-to-student ratio and hands-on instruction help students receive personalized support and meaningful attention. Every child is known by name and never lost in the crowd.
